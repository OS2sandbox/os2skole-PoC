# Managing Risk

Documents and Planning related to risks we encounter during development.

## IAM Component

Folder: [./iam](./iam/README.md)

Managing user identities system-wide presents a high risk (medium likelihood, high impact). We are therefore drafting parts of the IAM architecture to catch potential pitfalls early.

## Delivery Pipeline

Folder: [./delivery](./delivery/README.md)

The delivery of updates will be a challenge to the quality of our inter-vendor procedures. It presents a high risk (high likelihood, medium impact). Being able to get early feedback from CI/CD experts and vendors is crucial.
